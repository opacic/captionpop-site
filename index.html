About the app

Make your video easy to follow.

CaptionPop automatically creates timed captions that you can review, edit and style before sharing. Built for short videos, with a direct path from the original clip to the finished export.

In the 1.4 test release

Captions you can edit

Generate timed captions from the original speech. Correct words, highlight key phrases, choose a font and preset, and place the captions where they work best.

Visuals with their own timing

Add emoji, stickers, icons and your own images or logos. Move, resize and rotate them, then choose when they appear.

Sound and video looks

Add short sound effects, adjust their volume and timing, and give the video a vintage or another color look.

Frame your next post

Choose a video format, including portrait for Reels and Shorts. Fit or fill the frame and review the result in preview.

Keep up to three drafts

Free, Clean or SRT

A few things to know

Does Clean require a subscription?

No. Clean is a one-time Google Play purchase for the selected project. Check the price and currency shown by Google Play before confirming. A purchase does not transfer to a replacement draft.

Do drafts disappear after 24 hours?

Local drafts remain on your device until you delete or replace them. Temporary server copies are scheduled for deletion within 24 hours; reopening a draft does not renew that window. Keep your original video and save exports you want to retain.

Will translation change my audio?

No. The upcoming translation feature changes caption text. The original video and spoken audio remain unchanged; it is not voice dubbing.

What happens when all three draft slots are full?

Choose a draft to replace. The previous draft stays safe until the new video finishes processing and saving. An unfinished replacement can be resumed or discarded.
