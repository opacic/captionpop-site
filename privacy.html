Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how CapPops processes information when you use the Android app.

1. Who operates CapPops

CapPops is operated by SMALL TASKS. Privacy questions can be sent to support@captionpop.app. Website: https://captionpop.app.

2. No CapPops account

CapPops does not require or create a CapPops user account. We do not ask you to create a username or password for the service. Google Play may use your Google account to process a purchase, but CapPops does not receive your payment-card details.

3. Information CapPops processes

Depending on the features you use, CapPops may process:

  • Video content that you select or record and choose to process.
  • Audio extracted from the selected video for speech transcription. Caption text may also be sent to OpenAI for requested translation or suggested word emphasis, when these features are available in your release.
  • Caption text, including automatically generated captions, edits you make and translations when that feature is available in your release.
  • Visual and audio additions, including images or logos you add, their placement and timing, and custom text when available, as needed to render your requested video.
  • Support reports you choose to send, including your description and relevant app version, Android version, device model, time and the issue details you include. The app prepares the report for you to review and send.
  • Technical identifiers, such as a random installation identifier, project/job identifiers and security tokens used to authorize your project and prevent abuse.
  • Purchase information, such as a Google Play product identifier, purchase token/confirmation and entitlement status for a Clean export.
  • Basic network and operational data, such as IP/network metadata and server logs that may be processed for security, reliability, rate limiting, fraud prevention and troubleshooting.

4. Screen recording and device permissions

When you record your screen in CapPops, Android asks for screen-capture consent for each recording session. If you choose microphone or device-audio capture, the relevant audio permission is used for that recording. CapPops also uses the Android “Display over other apps” capability to show its movable recording control. The overlay permission itself does not cause CapPops to collect unrelated screen content.

A recording is first created in app-private storage on your device. It is uploaded for processing only after you choose to use the selected clip.

5. Why the information is used

CapPops uses the information above to provide the service you request, including uploading a selected clip, transcribing speech, generating and editing captions, translating caption text when requested and available, rendering Free or Clean exports, delivering downloads, verifying one-time purchases, preventing abuse, securing the service and diagnosing failures.

6. Service providers

For speech transcription, audio may be sent to ElevenLabs or, if there is a technical failure, to AssemblyAI through its EU endpoint. Earlier test releases used OpenAI for speech transcription. OpenAI may also process caption text for a translation or word-emphasis suggestion you request when those features are available in your release.

Google Play processes in-app purchases and provides purchase verification information. Cloudflare provides hosting, request handling, queues and temporary media storage. Supabase provides database hosting for application records, including project and entitlement state. These providers process information under their own terms and applicable data-protection obligations.

CapPops does not sell uploaded videos, caption text or personal data, and does not use uploaded videos for advertising.

7. Retention and deletion

Server-side copies of uploaded source media, extracted audio, generated caption artifacts and rendered media are temporary. They are scheduled for automatic deletion after the processing/download window, within 24 hours. Opening or editing a local draft does not extend the server retention window. If an operation is already actively finishing at the cutoff, cleanup may run immediately after that operation safely settles.

Limited technical, security, anti-abuse and purchase/entitlement records may be kept longer where necessary to operate the service, prevent fraud, restore a paid entitlement, meet accounting obligations or comply with law. Those records do not require us to keep your source video.

Local drafts, including their saved edits, remain under your control until you delete or replace the project, clear CapPops app data, or uninstall the app. Version 1.4.1 allows up to five local drafts. Videos or SRT files that you explicitly save to shared device storage are separate copies and are not deleted automatically by CapPops.

For information about deletion requests, see Data deletion.

8. Security

CapPops uses HTTPS for production network traffic, non-public project authorization tokens, restricted server credentials and access controls intended to protect temporary media and service data. No method of transmission or storage is completely risk-free, but CapPops is designed to limit access to data needed to provide the requested service.

9. Your choices and rights

You may stop using CapPops at any time, delete local app data through Android, uninstall the app and delete any exported files you saved to your device. Because CapPops has no user accounts, there is no CapPops account to close.

Depending on where you live, you may also have legal rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection to processing. For an active processing job, include the project/job identifier when available so we can locate the relevant temporary data.

10. Children

CapPops is not designed specifically for children. The app should not be used by a person who is below the minimum age at which they may lawfully consent to use an online service in their jurisdiction without appropriate parental or guardian involvement.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, its infrastructure or legal requirements change. The date at the top of this page identifies the current version.
