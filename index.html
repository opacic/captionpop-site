About the app

Make your video easy to follow.

CapPops automatically creates timed captions that you can review, edit and style before sharing. Built for short videos, with a direct path from the original clip to the finished export.

In the 1.4.1 test release

Captions you can edit

Generate timed captions from the original speech. Correct words, highlight key phrases, choose a font and preset, and place the captions where they work best.

Visuals with their own timing

Add emoji, stickers, icons and your own images or logos. Move, resize and rotate them, then choose when they appear.

Sound and video looks

Add short sound effects, adjust their volume and timing, and give the video a vintage or another color look.

Frame your next post

Choose a video format, including portrait for Reels and Shorts. Fit or fill the frame and review the result in preview.

Keep up to five drafts

Return to projects saved on your device. Local drafts stay until you delete or replace them. Server copies expire separately.

Free, Clean or SRT

Export a free video with a watermark, unlock Clean for that project with a one-time purchase, or save the captions as an SRT file.

No CapPops account or subscription required.

From a clip to a finished video

  1. Choose your video

    Select a clip up to three minutes, or record your screen on a supported Android device.

  2. Review and make it yours

    Check the original captions, adjust the style and framing, and add visuals or sound.

  3. Preview, then export

    Review the final video before saving it. Google Play shows your local Clean price before purchase.

Keep the original. Add a translation.

In version 1.4.1, you can save translations alongside your original captions and choose which language to show in your export.

Up to three translations

Choose from 148 language options and keep up to three translations alongside the original in one draft. The editor clearly identifies which captions you are editing and exporting. Original audio stays unchanged.

Fonts for different scripts

Expanded font support adapts to the text while retaining preset colors and effects. Long captions can use shorter timed pages.

Your own text visuals

Add custom text with an app font and an optional rounded background. Move, resize, rotate and choose its display time using the Visual controls.

A few things to know

How many videos can I process for free?

Version 1.4.1 includes one new Free video per rolling 24 hours and up to three different Free export versions per project. Saving the same finished version again does not use another version.

Does Clean require a subscription?

No. Clean is a one-time Google Play purchase for the selected project. Check the price and currency shown by Google Play before confirming. A purchase does not transfer to a replacement draft.

Do drafts disappear after 24 hours?

Local drafts remain on your device until you delete or replace them. Temporary server copies are scheduled for deletion within 24 hours; reopening a draft does not renew that window. Keep your original video and save exports you want to retain.

Will translation change my audio?

Translation changes the caption text. Your spoken audio stays as recorded.

What happens when all five draft slots are full?

Choose a draft to replace. The previous draft stays safe until the new video finishes processing and saving. You can resume or discard an unfinished replacement.
